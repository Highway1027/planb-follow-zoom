# Follow Zoom

A Plan B Terraform mod. When the camera follows a vehicle, the mouse wheel and zoom keys zoom in and out instead of ending the follow. Right or middle mouse still stops following.

Needs BepInEx 5 (5.4.23.5 tested). Install: copy `FollowZoom.dll` to `<game folder>\BepInEx\plugins\FollowZoom\`. It stores nothing in your save; remove the DLL to go back to vanilla.
